Caime is messaging that understands your relationships.

Say how you know someone (family, a friend, a colleague, your dentist) and Caime keeps your conversations with them in that light: what needs you comes first, the rest waits quietly, and nobody else ever sees how you labelled them.

What people ask and promise in a chat becomes something you can keep: a request becomes an action with its day, a plan to meet becomes a meeting card both sides agree to, and an amount becomes a Pay card, a record between you (Caime never holds or moves money).

Cai, your assistant, helps when you ask: it catches you up on what you missed, drafts a reply, answers from your own conversations with the lines it read, and takes notes in a call when everyone in it says yes. What it suggests stays a suggestion until you accept it.

Calls, one to one or with up to eight people. Private conversations are end-to-end encrypted: Caime's servers never see their words.

Organizations prove who they are with their own web domain, and answer customers in one inbox, with bookings, orders and forms as cards in the conversation.

Bring your chats with you: export a chat from the messaging app you use now and open it in Caime.

In English, Arabic, French and Turkish.
