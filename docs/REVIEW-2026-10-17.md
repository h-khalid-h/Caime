# Whole-product challenge review — 2026-10-17

The owner asked for a review that challenges the entire product, not its code alone: customer
journeys, adoption and friction, usability, business value, trust, safety and privacy,
integrations and automation, intelligence and Cai, architecture, stability, scale and
performance, mail, calendar, chat, calls, meetings, files, cards, apps, public pages, UX, UI and
copy, normalization and personalization, age verification, and readiness, so that a person or an
organization using Caime in full needs no Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, WhatsApp or
Signal, even while Caime integrates with them. The owner also asked that an Integration
Framework (registry, credentials, mapping, reliable sync, monitoring, governance) come before
more connectors, and governed AI over all of Caime's and the integrations' data after it.

Ten read-only slices ran in parallel over the code at `87fc778` (R96), each with a narrow brief
and evidence per finding; this is their synthesis and the plan (ROADMAP M16). The previous
reviews are `REVIEW-2026-10-09.md`, `REVIEW-2026-10-05.md` and `REVIEW-2026-10.md`; what they
fixed isn't repeated. Facts about the code were produced by running things today: 316 commits;
40 feature areas in the app, 134 server libraries, 55 route modules, 89 migrations; 227 test
files and 30 E2E specs; the full gate green at `87fc778` (1,942 unit and API tests, 123 E2E,
startup bundle 448.0 of 450 KB gzip). Days in the plan are estimates, not measurements.

Severity: **H** wrong, leaking or blocking adoption now; **M** degrades with use or misleads;
**L** polish. Paths are repository-relative; line numbers are from the review's commit.

## 0. The verdict

**Where Caime is genuinely ahead today.** Relationship labels decide what needs you and when
you're told, and every placement says why; nothing else does this. One identity carries personal
and business life, and the customer's side of a business is an ordinary conversation with
bookings, orders and Pay cards in it, not a CRM tab. Message intelligence works by rules first in
four languages, Arabic varieties included, and every inference is a suggestion with a quoted
reason. Data rights for an organization (export, erasure, retention, a DPA) are built in, which a
clinic can't get from WhatsApp Business. The AI agent always says it's an AI and hands over to
people. Performance is budgeted and measured. The price is a fraction of the alternatives'.

**The honest gap to "no Workspace, Slack or WhatsApp needed".** Caime can replace Slack plus
Calendly for a small team today, and WhatsApp for a group of people who all join. It cannot yet
replace Workspace or Microsoft 365: mail on an organization's own domain, documents and sheets, a
files place, meetings above eight people, an admin console. The matrix:

| Alternative | A solo professional | A 10-person clinic | A family |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp | Yes, once contacts join: chat, voice, groups, calls (web). Blocked by: no store app and no phone push yet (⛔), finding people by number needs the WhatsApp sender (⛔) | Partly: the customer side yes; staff chat yes; no WhatsApp Business inbox or reminders by WhatsApp/SMS | Yes once everyone joins; blocked by the store app and group invite links |
| Signal | Yes for private conversations on the web (E2EE, device trust, recovery); phones built, untried | Same | Same |
| Slack | Yes: spaces, topics, mentions, attention; better notifications. Blocked by: no team invite link, free team of 3, no apps posting into spaces | Yes for the team; blocked by team invites and apps in spaces | n/a |
| Calendly | Nearly: bookings, catalog, hours, branches. Blocked by: booking needs an account (6 fields), no Book link to share, no "block time" | Same, plus card payments live (⛔ Connect) | n/a |
| Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 | No: mail only at `@cai.me`, no own domain; no docs or drive | No | n/a |

**Three findings run through every slice.**

1. **The code is ahead of production.** Owner-side ⛔ dependencies now sit inside core journeys:
   SMTP (password reset, meeting guests, mail), the Google and Microsoft OAuth scopes (R90, R96),
   Stripe Connect, the WhatsApp sender (phone numbers, contacts match), the Picker key, store
   accounts, hosting region. A visitor hits several of these in their first ten minutes. §1 and
   §9 list where each bites; §13 lists the owner's decisions.
2. **Breadth outran the launch.** On 2026-10-04 the roadmap said nothing was left to build before a
   first customer; since then 169 commits and R57–R96 added orders, stock, a marketplace, mail, a
   handle engine, places, calendar sync, while the P0 launch items didn't move. The wedge still
   holds; the surface has outgrown the goal. The plan (§12) puts a launch floor and adoption
   before any new area.
3. **Drift where a guard is narrower than its rule, and logic written twice.** `selectAll('u')`
   passes the columns test (11 sites, the inbox and every conversation open among them); the
   i18n literal scanner misses strings assigned to variables (English shows through on the chat
   list, calls and reports in Arabic, French and Turkish); `keys.test.ts` misses 23 hand-written
   keys in `realtime/apply.ts`. When a card ends is computed in seven places with two answers;
   a transcript is built four ways; `clip` six; `PUBLIC_URL` is trimmed at 21 sites, not at two.

## 1. Customer journeys and adoption (slice 1)

A web sign-up to a first conversation with someone not on Caime is about six screens, and value
arrives only when the other person joins. A clinic owner's path to "first customer booked and
paid" is twelve or more screens, and nothing on the way in recognises a business owner. A
stranger on a public Book link meets five screens and twelve inputs before anything is booked.

- **H1 "From your contacts" leads onboarding and does almost nothing on iPhone web or desktop.**
  The web picker exists only in Chrome on Android (`apps/app/src/features/people/contacts/local.ts:1-5,27`);
  Google and Outlook contacts are ⛔; matching by number needs a proven number, which needs the
  WhatsApp sender (⛔). *Fix:* on a browser without a picker, lead with "Invite by WhatsApp or SMS".
- **H2 Nothing on the way in recognises a business owner.** `/business` "Start free" goes to a
  plain sign-up (`lib/site-pages.ts:603`); onboarding has no "for my organization" branch
  (`onboarding.tsx:63,101-108`); creating an organization is four taps deep in Settings. *Fix:*
  the CTA carries `?link=/orgs/new`; onboarding asks "For me / For my organization".
- **H3 Staff can't be invited.** A team member must already have an account *and* a connection
  with the owner (`modules/orgs.ts:628-638`); no email or link invite, no join by verified domain.
  *Fix:* a team invite link and email, joining on sign-up; domain auto-join once verified.
- **H4 Slots are hidden until after sign-up**, and the Book button goes straight to sign-up
  (`lib/public-pages.ts:1343,1348`; `modules/calendar.ts:363-364`). Calendly shows times first,
  then asks for a name and email. *Fix:* public slots on the page; book with name plus email or
  phone as a provisional person (the mechanism meeting guests already use, `lib/provisional.ts`),
  claimed later; three fields on a link arrival.
- **H5 Groups can't be brought over.** Spaces and groups admit only members' connections
  (`modules/spaces.ts:4,87-88`); no group or space join link; the WhatsApp importer refuses
  groups and iPhone's `.zip`. Slack has no path at all, and the free team is 3. *Fix:* space and
  group join links (approval optional); accept `.zip`.
- **H6 Meeting guests can't be invited in production** (needs mail, `lib/meeting-guests.ts:432`),
  and the organizer is never shown the `/j` link to copy. *Fix:* "Copy guest link".
- **H7 iPhone web users get no notifications and aren't told why** (`push/support.ts:12-16`). *Fix:*
  an "Add to Home Screen to be notified" card on Home.
- **M** Onboarding shares the weak `/@handle` link, not an invite link that connects at once
  (`onboarding.tsx:271-276`); a person's page promises "Message", the app then offers "Connect";
  "Forgot password" promises a link the server refuses without SMTP (`auth.ts:458`); DNS-only
  verification stalls the setup checklist forever (`setupSteps.ts:9`); there is no Book link or
  QR beside the door; no "block time" or holidays; the sign-up form loses the pending link's
  context and says "half a minute" over six required fields; an expired invite renders as
  "Nobody by that handle"; the guest page offers only Sign out.
- **Good:** the pending link survives sign-up and onboarding; the door lands a customer in the
  conversation in under 60 s (E2E); invite links connect at once and carry context; contacts not on
  Caime are invited one tap each from the person's own WhatsApp, SMS or mail.

## 3. Age assurance (the owner's agemin.com question)

**What Agemin is, from what's public.** A product of Bynn Intelligence: facial age *estimation*
(no documents), offered through an API, described as privacy-preserving. The company claims a
mean absolute error of 0.9 years for ages 9–21 and 1.8 for adults, with a NIST FATE/AEV
submission planned at the time of the announcement and enterprise availability slated for Q4
2025. Its site didn't answer what it retains or where it processes. Before contracting, verify
the NIST result, the retention terms (images deleted after the estimate, no template kept), the
processing region and a DPA; the same questions go to any alternative (Yoti, Private ID's
on-device estimation, didit). The design below works with any of them.

**The design.** Levels on the account: `self_declared` (today's date of birth) →
`estimated_adult` / `estimated_minor` (a face estimate, age band and confidence only) →
`document_verified`. Caime never stores an image or a biometric template: the provider gets a
one-time session, Caime keeps the verdict, band, confidence, provider and date, and the audit
chain gets a row without any image. The provider sits behind an `AgeProvider` interface beside
`lib/provider-health.ts` with a signed-webhook result (the Stripe pattern), is listed in
`packages/core/src/processors.ts` and the DPA, and the privacy page names it and says no image
is kept. `minorOf` reads the level, so the ~45 gates inherit it; the SQL rule in `people.ts`
reads a stored `is_minor`. **When it's asked:** before paid items, checkout, billing, Connect,
running or joining an organization, meeting guests, OAuth apps and tokens, mail to outside
addresses; before connecting with or calling a non-connection (an invite link, a stranger's
request); on a report that names someone's age; and on accounts declaring 13–17 that search for
other minors. A country's law can demand a level (Egypt and Saudi Arabia: verified adult). An
estimate that contradicts the declared age moves the account to the stricter tier at once, with
appeal by document; a failed adult check grants nothing and changes nothing; retries are
rate-limited. "Age checked" is a privacy field of its own and in the export. *Declined:* blanket
verification of every sign-up (friction without a risk to answer), storing images, and inferring
age from a face without the person asking for a level.

## 4. Architecture, reliability, performance (slices 3, 4, 10)

**Solid:** `SKIP LOCKED` job claims woken by NOTIFY, dedupe keys and capped backoff; sweeps that
claim rows; Stripe handled once per event and re-read from Stripe; timeouts on every outbound
fetch; migrations under an advisory lock with checksums; bounded in-process caches; fixed-label
metrics; module boundaries enforced; CI with real Postgres, E2E, audit and an image smoke test.

**Reliability (H).**
- **H1 Realtime dies silently after a database restart.** The bus reconnects once after 1 s and
  swallows failure (`lib/bus.ts:48-52`); the errored client is never released; sockets stay open;
  `/v1/readyz` runs only `select 1`. *Fix:* retry forever with backoff, release, a `resync` frame,
  bus health in readyz.
- **H2 Nobody is alerted about anything.** Alert rules exist on paper (DEPLOY.md); the uptime
  check and receiver are ⛔; no error tracking; the request id never leaves the server
  (`app.ts:150`). *Fix:* a receiver, Sentry on server and app, `x-request-id` echoed, log shipping.
- **H3 Backups sit on the volume with the uploads; a restore was never run on production;**
  `copyOffHost` reads the whole dump into memory (`lib/backup.ts:107`). *Fix:* `BACKUP_S3_*`,
  `s3PutStream`, a timed restore drill.
- **H4 Pool exhaustion stalls an instance.** No `connectionTimeoutMillis`, `statement_timeout` or
  `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` (`db/pool.ts:16`); the resumable upload holds a
  transaction and row lock while streaming 16 MB from the client (`modules/files.ts:259-275`);
  and the booking check inside the send transaction opens a second pool connection and may wait
  up to 4 s on a calendar read (`lib/messages.ts:1211-1234`, R96's `freshenBusy`). *Fix:* temp file
  first; freshen before the transaction and re-check inside it on `trx`; the timeouts.
- **M** Jobs have no time limit or heartbeat (a lock older than 5 min runs twice; shutdown doesn't
  wait; 8 shared slots let AI starve mail); no dead-letter (an exhausted job is deleted silently
  after 7 days and keeps `CaimeJobsStuck` firing); `billing.reconcile` blocks the loop, unlocked,
  on every instance; message effects and push are in-memory best effort (`ctx.defer`); migrations
  run in one transaction (no `CONCURRENTLY`, no `lock_timeout`; 0066, 0067 and 0089 backfill
  `messages` whole; 0035 dropped columns in the release that replaced them; five committed
  migrations were edited); the tested image isn't the deployed image (EasyPanel rebuilds from
  source, `:latest` only, no rollback, no staging); exports are built in memory; one health check
  serves liveness and readiness. **Multi-instance:** presence first/last socket, `inOrder` effect
  ordering (double notifications), rate limits ×N, i18n language cache, AI and TURN caches ×N,
  and backup/audit/provider gauges empty on a web-only instance (false `CaimeBackupOld`).

**Performance (H).**
- **H5 A large group's message costs thousands of queries and groups have no cap:** delivered acks
  ~5 queries per device (`modules/conversations.ts:658-737`), notifications 3.4 per member, every
  device rebuilding its inbox after a burst (`realtime/apply.ts:122-133`); a 500-member space
  is ~4,000 queries and 500 inbox rebuilds per message. A voice transcript rebuilds the view once
  per member (`lib/transcribe.ts:128-136`). *Fix:* a large-group mode above ~100 (no acks, one
  batched insert, patch not refetch), one view published once.
- **H6 People lists run queries per row:** `GET /connections` 1 + 2N with no limit
  (`modules/connections.ts:310-313`), requests the same, people search up to ~400 concurrent
  queries per keystroke (`people.ts:303-312`), org search a seat per org. `personViewsFor` already
  does it in a fixed number and claims these callers. *Fix:* use it; a scale test with a ceiling.
- **H7 The inbox has scans `inbox-scale` can't see:** `pending` returns every question since my
  last message with a correlated `max(seq)` per row (`modules/inbox.ts:146-176`); `my_last_seq`
  scans a conversation I never posted in; the test seeds 40 messages. *Fix:* `participants.last_sent_seq`
  written in the send transaction, `DISTINCT ON` per conversation.
- **H8 `selectAll` on aliased tables passes the guard** (`columns.test.ts:51`, `messaging.test.ts:1668`):
  the inbox ships both search vectors per row (`modules/inbox.ts:143`), every conversation open
  ships the catalog per member (`lib/conversation-views.ts:145`), nine more. *Fix:* the guard
  matches aliases, an allowlist per function; fix the eleven.
- **M** `ctx.defer` has no bound or gauge; bus payloads over 7 KB go through a table (170 ids:
  typing re-reads members per tick, presence up to 1,000 recipients); `freshenBusy` starts up to 50
  reads at once and the sweep's 50 per 15 min goes stale past 50 accounts; `updates.changed` makes
  every follower refetch within 400 ms; images have no concurrency cap and default
  `limitInputPixels`; orders and Cai's counts scan every card by `payload->>'kit'`; the device cache
  has no byte cap. **Bundle:** 448.0 of 450 KB; `endpoints.ts` (4.3 KB, 293 endpoints) and SVG
  parsing sit in `__common`; `fr` and `tr` (81 KB) need the split `ar` has.

## 5. Intelligence and Cai (slice 5)

Rules first, four languages, Arabic varieties, suggestions with a quoted reason and words for
confidence, learning that can be forgotten, initiative in three steps, four free questions,
briefs, digest, follow-ups sent only on a tap, search that reads sentences. Governance holds for
a person: nothing a model produces is applied without an explicit accept, and payments and
cancellations are never the agent's.

- **M Sources are linked in two places only** (Ask Cai in Search, Find actions); catch-up, ask a
  conversation, Cai and Friend chat and the brief summary return plain text (`lib/ai.ts:298-312`,
  `system-accounts.ts:478-498`, `briefs.ts:475`); Cai's rule answers are untappable lines.
- **M The agent decides alone where R87 says the team does:** it closes threads (`agent.ts:607-615`),
  its booking card is the team's side so only the customer can Confirm (`kit-cards.ts:162`) while
  it tells the customer the team will, and its orders take stock at once.
- **M Private content reaches the model:** Ask Cai in Search sends task and decision titles with no
  private-conversation filter (`modules/search.ts:424-498`); the server lets a decision be recorded
  in a private conversation (`modules/actions.ts:550-560`), only the app hides it (R18, R67).
- **H No evaluation of any model-backed path:** `ai.test.ts` checks plumbing with a stand-in; no
  faithfulness, find-actions recall, agent-policy or injection suite; prompts unversioned.
- **M Measurement:** the due-date floor is pooled across languages (Arabic has ~9 dated lines of
  63, so every one could be wrong); 48 Arabic lines in all, 4 Arabic decision positives, no Gulf
  or Maghrebi dialect lines; amounts, `chatIntent` and search unmeasured; "Quite sure" hand-set.
- **M Cost:** allowance per call not per token; search spends it silently after a one-second pause
  (`app/search.tsx:84-99`); only the agent uses prompt caching; everything runs on the top model
  with `ANTHROPIC_MODEL_LIGHT` unset; the agent's run record omits cache tokens.
- **Gaps that matter.** For a person: no "draft a reply", no recap across conversations, no
  meeting or call recap, no reading of files, Cai not in groups, Cai can't propose a card. For a
  clinic: no review-before-send mode, no on/off per action, no office hours, no citations to its
  knowledge (one 8,000-character text), no report of unanswered questions. Voice notes are never
  analysed (`transcribe.ts:116-135`) and read as "Voice message" to every model path. Qualifiers
  are dropped ("what am I waiting for *from Ana*" lists every wait). Nothing can answer "what did
  Ana and I agree, is it in my calendar, did she pay": no decisions, cards with state, payments or
  integration data reach Cai.

## 6. Mail, calendar, meetings, calls, files (slice 6)

Caime handles the relationship side of each better than the incumbents; what's missing is the
everyday mechanics.

- **Mail (R75).** Exists: authenticated receiving, strangers as requests, threads as topics,
  attachments, HTML viewer, folded signatures, seen and opened states, notices, a subject line,
  writing to any address, the agent answering by mail. Missing, most hit first: **CC and reply-all**

  sender check is dropped silently** (`mail-in.ts:111`, against R75's promised notice; the owner

  **calendar invitations arrive as a bare file**; **cards are offered in mail conversations but
  never mailed** (`mail-out.ts:60`; `KitForm.tsx:96-114` doesn't hide them); no own address or
  organization domain (an English "Open in Caime" footer on every mail); plain text out, no
  signature, attachments over 10 MB skipped silently; no scheduled send, snooze, mark unread,

- **Calendar.** Exists: agenda by day, Join and Open for Zoom and Meet links, the ICS feed, Plans

  **guests' calendars go stale** (sequence always 0, no cancellation sent, `meeting-guests.ts:386-390`);

- **Files.** Exists: resumable uploads to 100 MB, storage by plan, per-conversation media, files

  folders, share by link with permissions, Office previews, collaborative docs**; trash and
  restore, versions, full-text search in files.
- **Where surfaces don't connect:** a meeting proposed in a mail thread is never mailed, can't be
  accepted, stays "asked" and is pushed to Google as tentative for good; customers who write by
  mail never get their appointment or Pay cards; a meeting space's messages never reach its email
  guests; a call has no link to its card (`calls` has no message id); invitations mailed to a

**Build order** (each step makes the next cheaper, and the first two are unsafe to leave):
1. Credentials (b): the cost grows with every refresh token collected.
2. Per-use health, probes, alerts (e): nothing can be operated while it can't be seen.
3. The connector interface with stand-ins, dead-letter, `Retry-After`, single-run sweeps (a, d).
4. Governance (f): required before any organization or enterprise pitch.
5. The contract: real response schemas, webhooks in OpenAPI, a forward-compatible SDK, a versioning
   and sandbox policy, publishing to npm.
6. A person event feed (`after=` cursor) and personal webhooks: fixes the bridges and gives recipes
   their trigger bus.
7. Recipes (conversation and space automations), then organization-installed apps, posting as an
   app into a space, slash commands as signed deliveries, card moves everywhere.
8. Governed AI over everything.

**Governed AI, concretely.** `sources[]` on suggestions, Cai's answers and `ai_runs` (kind,
provider, external id, fetched-at, hash) so every sentence opens what it came from; a prompt
version on every run; `text()` moved to the structured `{answer, sources}` shape
`answerFromFound` already uses; a suggestion kind for external actions (write to a calendar,
send a mail, change a booking) with a payload and an idempotency key, run through the connector
only when the person taps (the `sendFollowUp` pattern); the agent's reply, booking, order and
close as pending suggestions for the team in a review mode; audit rows for proposed, approved and
executed with the source hashes in the chain; sources deleted with the account and on erasure.
Reading Google or Outlook event *details* for Cai partly reverses R90's "when, never what" and is
the owner's call: if taken, an opt-in "event details" use, read on demand, kept briefly.
