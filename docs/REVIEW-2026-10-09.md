# Whole-product review — 2026-10-09

The owner asked for a deep review of the product, architecture, code, UX, design, performance,
scale and fragmentation before the next block (country-aware UX, consistent pickers, Caime Mail).
Six read-only slices ran in parallel over the code as of `21f2402` (R74), each with a narrow
brief and evidence per finding; this is their synthesis and the plan. The previous review is
`REVIEW-2026-10.md` (2026-10-05); what it fixed isn't repeated.

Severity: **H** wrong or leaking now; **M** degrades with use or misleads; **L** polish.
Paths are repository-relative; line numbers are from the review's commit.

## 1. Security and privacy (slice 5, with slices 3 and 4 where they agree)

- **H1 Private waits reach the person they're about.** The rule everywhere else is "owner is
  me, or assignee is me and `shared`"; three readers drop `shared`: Cai and the friends
  (`lib/system-accounts.ts:572`, counts `:596-604`, the copy sent to the model `:420,:476`, the
  morning brief `lib/cai.ts:271`), the meeting brief (`lib/briefs.ts:245-253`) and the inbox's
  overdue/due-soon counts (`modules/inbox.ts:182-187`, which moves a conversation to Needs you).
  The only assignee index is partial on `shared`, so the OR branch scans too. Fix: one
  `visibleTasksWhere(me)` in `lib/`, used by every task query.
- **M1 A card payment can be lost or charged twice.** `openCheckout` replaces a session Stripe
  doesn't report `open`, without a row lock or a compare (`lib/checkout.ts:245-256,291-295`);
  `settleCheckout` and the webhook ask only about the current session; a paid-but-unsettled
  session is overwritten; two team members paying one org-to-org card overwrite each other;
  sessions aren't expired on received/cancelled. Fix: under the card's row lock, settle the
  current session first and refuse if complete; replace only once expired; expire the replaced.
- **M2 `GET /people/:id/slots` without `item`** shows anyone signed in a year of a person's free
  and busy times (`modules/calendar.ts:259-272,394-418`), skipping item audiences and the
  profile gate; contradicts SECURITY.md. Fix: nothing unless the item is in `itemsFor`.
- **M3 Team ids leak through items' `providers`** to any signed-in viewer
  (`modules/orgs.ts:193-199`; core `booking.ts:351`); a customer may pick `ask.providerId`
  (`lib/booking.ts:345`). Fix: strip without a seat; ignore `providerId` unless `isSelf`.
- **M4 `payload.checkout.payerId` passes the business mask** (`lib/checkout.ts:285-295`;
  `maskPayload` `lib/business.ts:131-182`). Fix: map a non-customer payer to `orgId`.
- **M5 A priced booking can reach a minor** (the team's booker is the adult member,
  `lib/messages.ts:314`; `appointment` isn't `adultsOnly`). Fix: the all-adults check for a
  priced item.
- **M6 Writing as an organization and its checkout outlive the authority**: `asOrgId` checked
  only at the thread's start; `payToFor` reads `customer_org_id` with no seat or open check
  (`lib/messages.ts:391-402`); `closeOrg` never disconnects `org_checkout`; `checkoutAccountOf`
  ignores `archived_at`. Fix: seat and open checks on every send and in `payToFor`;
  `closeOrg` disconnects.
- **M7 An approved listing isn't necessarily what the operator saw**: edits while waiting keep
  `listed_at`; approval has no version (`modules/oauth.ts:235-245`, `modules/admin.ts:560-575`);
  the operator can't see a pending icon. Fix: bump `listed_at` on a change to what's shown, the
  review carries the `listedAt` it saw, an operator icon route.
- **L** Discover keeps an organization as publisher after the seat ends or it closes
  (`lib/app-directory.ts:59-62,87`); the provider reassign route lacks `assertCanWrite` and a
  limit (`modules/messages.ts:410-447`); a malformed `payload.booking`/`order` is a 500
  (`lib/messages.ts:882,884`); the Connect return isn't tied to the starting session
  (`modules/checkout.ts:104-119`, audit by handle `:124-128`); an OAuth app's `website` takes any
  scheme (`core schemas.ts:356`, opened by `AppSheet.tsx:99`); the brief includes messages the
  reader deleted for themselves (`lib/briefs.ts:274-295`); strangers can burn a reclaim's tries
  (`modules/orgs.ts:790,810`); an item photo or app icon without a rendition serves the original
  with its metadata (`modules/files.ts:617-624`; require `thumb_key`); clients store arbitrary
  JSON on text messages (`lib/messages.ts:797`).
- Sound: the checkout routes and webhook, the kit move route, `PATCH /me/oauth-apps/:id`, the
  directory and icon routes, item photo routes, Cai's routes, the system accounts' fences,
  `/attention`, the booking PUTs, invites, the admin people routes, the calendar feed.

## 2. Cross-integration and fragmentation (slice 3)

- **H2 A team member's calendar shows every appointment the organization has** (every member is
  a participant in every customer conversation, `modules/business.ts:215-224`; `cardsAhead` has no
  provider filter, only `openSlotsFor` does, `lib/booking.ts:231-247`): the in-app calendar, the
  ICS feed, Attention's coming up, Cai, `busyNow`, and the profile's `busyDetails` to friends.
  Fix: the provider filter into `cardsAhead` (`lib/upcoming.ts`).
- **H3 Deleting an account deletes an organization's logo and catalog photos** (the orphan
  query spares only `message_files` and `album_photos`, `modules/account.ts:78-102,131-139`).
  Fix: spare files an organization references.
- **H4 Discover lists under a closed organization, with Verified**, and under one the developer
  left (`lib/app-directory.ts:87`; `closeOrg` and leaving never touch `oauth_clients.org_id`).
  Fix: clear `org_id` there and join open organizations only.
- **H5 Search can't find cards or organizations**: `messages.search` indexes `body` only and
  cards have none; organizations only through `relationships.org_name`; `period` filters
  `created_at`, not a card's date (`modules/search.ts:56-192`). Fix: index a card's title,
  reference and names; match organizations through business threads; dated cards through
  `calendarItems`.
- **M8 Consent, Connected and Discover disagree on who made an app and its icon**
  (`OAuthConsentView` has neither; Connected names the developer; Discover the organization;
  the icon route 404s for a connected unlisted app). Fix: one publisher-and-icon helper in

- **M9 A developer isn't told the review decision and sees no usage** (`modules/admin.ts:560-586`;
  `OAuthAppView` lacks `connectedCount`). Fix: a notification and `connectedCount`.
- **M10 The developer story is three places**: Developer never shows organization apps,
  `OrgApps` never shows listings under the organization, an organization's own app can't be in
  Discover, docs say "Apps → Add" on the page but it's on Setup. Fix: `OrgApps` lists the
  organization's listings with a link; docs name Setup.
- **M11 A card paid through Checkout skips what a hand-made paid move does** (no notification,
  no `kit.moved`, no webhook: `lib/checkout.ts:366-375` vs `modules/messages.ts:336-366`). Fix:
  one `afterCardMove` in `lib/kits.ts`.
- **M12 Organization apps don't hear bookings, orders or payments** (`business.message` carries
  no card; `kit.*` only for the app's own cards; no calendar route in `API_ROUTES`). Fix: the
  card in `business.message`, `kit.moved` for Caime's cards in customer threads,
  `GET /v1/orgs/:id/calendar` under `inbox:read`, SDK and docs.
- **M13 Exports miss data since 0045**: a person's `ordering`, `collections`, `payments`,
  `cai_follow_up` tasks, `learning_reset`; an organization's `customer_org_id`, catalog, offer
  settings, checkout, listings. Fix: `buildExport` and `buildOrgExport`.
- **M14 Cards that ask never "need you"** (`is_request = false`, `lib/messages.ts:1027`), and a
  Pay or invoice `due` isn't on the calendar. Fix: core `kit-cards` marks asking states as
  requests; `calendarItems` takes due cards.
- **M15 Realtime gaps**: `message.deleted`/`hidden` don't call `calendarChanged`; nothing is
  published on a grant, the feed, a review or booking hours. Fix: those calls and events.
- **M16 ICS and the app disagree on a booking's length** (`durationMinutes ?? 60` vs
  `minutesOf`). **M17 Three calendar entrances with no bridges**, Bookings doesn't name the
  customer's organization, Cai builds its own coming-up list. **M18 The SDK claims personal
  and OAuth tokens but has no person method**, and `me()` answers them `403`.
- **L** Reassigning a provider tells nobody; scope labels and several lines are English
  fragments; Connected's claim vs personal tokens in Developer; Discover offers Connect to
  under-18s and lists suspended developers' apps; `builtinApps` counts all feeds on every read;

- **M21 Kit-card reads scan all history** (`payload->'fields'->'start'->>'at'` with only

- **M26 An unbounded cache** (`summaries`, `lib/briefs.ts:181`) beside three capped ones. Fix:

## 4. Country awareness (slice 1)

Caime is partly country-aware: the country sets the work week at sign-up (and moves an
untouched one), the default currency (worked out from the country on each read), the Arabic
variety and one line in the AI prompt. Everything else ignores it. Country knowledge sits in
four places: core `time.ts:124-162` (the regions that don't work Mon–Fri; week start guessed
from the work week), core `arabic-variety.ts`, the server-only `lib/geo-data.ts` (countries,
zones, currencies, generated by `scripts/geo.mjs` from tzdata and CLDR) and a second partial
country-to-currency map inside core `amounts.ts`; currency decimals live twice.

- **H7 The amounts reader maps an ambiguous symbol to one currency** ("$" → USD, "ريال" → SAR,
  "درهم" → AED, "pounds" → GBP; `core amounts.ts:40-216`) and message analysis passes no country
  (`lib/messages.ts:568-579,775`); a kit form lets a typed currency override the country's
  (`KitForm.tsx:141-157`). **H8 Booking hours default to Mon–Fri 09–17 everywhere**, ignoring
  even `me.workweek` (`BookingSetup.tsx:179-214`).
- **M** Organizations have no work week or time zone (the booking zone is whoever edits it); the
  agent's prompt has no country, currency or week; `DayPicker` starts on Sunday with English day
  names (`DayPicker.tsx:8-52`); the morning brief runs on weekends; the formatting locale is
  independent of the country and a country change offers no zone or locale; ways to be paid are
  generic (`core payments.ts:14-24`); plan prices are one currency.
- **L** A hard-coded "EGP" placeholder; a 24-hour clock everywhere; the week start guessed;
  date pickers' first day from the OS; the booking hours line doesn't wrap past Saturday; the
  device's `firstWeekday`, `currencyCode` and `uses24hourClock` hints are never read; a free
  public item's JSON-LD says USD; phones only with a `+`; the two decimals tables disagree.

## 7. Plan

In this order, each block committed on its own, `pnpm check`, the budget and the E2E green
before each push, ROADMAP ticked only when verified end to end.

1. **Privacy and money (H1, M1–M7, the security Ls).** `visibleTasksWhere` for Cai, the briefs
   and the inbox; the checkout session settled under the row lock; slots gated; providers
   stripped; the payer masked; priced bookings adults-only; seats and open organizations checked
   on every send and in `payToFor`, `closeOrg` disconnecting the checkout; listing review by
   version with an operator icon; the Ls.
2. **Integration Hs (H2–H6).** The provider filter in `cardsAhead`; organization files spared on
   deletion; listings cleared of a closed or left organization; cards and organizations in
   search; slots from every overlapping card.
3. **Words (H9–H11, the i18n Ms).** `msg()` on the label tables; the friend's line; every
   fragment and bare literal a whole key with `trn` for counts; the catalogs; a collector that
   also flags a template literal mixing `tr` with English.
4. **Apps and Developer in harmony (M8–M12, the Ls).** One publisher-and-icon helper; the
   developer told and shown usage; `OrgApps` listing the organization's listings;
   `afterCardMove`; cards to organization apps and `GET /orgs/:id/calendar`; the SDK's person
   methods or an honest claim; Connect gated for minors; the counts kept, not counted.
5. **Scale (M19–M29).** The column lists; the booking lock; `card_start_at`; the module rule
   with its test; the pool, the NOTIFY and the jitter; the TTL cache; the sitemap cache; the
   splits; `AppListing` lazy.
6. **Design and a11y Ms**, the exports (M13), realtime (M15), the ICS length (M16), the
   calendar bridges (M17), the asking cards (M14).
7. **R76 country-aware UX**: `@caime/core/countries` generated from CLDR (currency and
   decimals, weekend, first day, work week, calling code, 24-hour clock, default zones, variety,
   pay kinds), the four tables folded in, derived fields on `MeView`/`OrgView`, organizations
   given a zone and a work week, and every caller in slice 1 reading it.
8. **R77 pickers**: the family (`Segmented` for views only, `ChoiceChips` with `multiple`,
   `ChoiceSheet`, `PickerField` + `SearchSheet`, `DateField`/`TimeField`/`WhenField` on one
   shell, `ListRow` drawing its mark, `SwitchRow`, `Stepper`) and every picker moved onto it,
   with per-option testIDs and the E2E queries updated.
9. **Docs drift**, then **R75 phase 1** on the design as refined in PRODUCT-REVIEW.
