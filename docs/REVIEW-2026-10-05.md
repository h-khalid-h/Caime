# Caime, reviewed from all angles: 2026-10-05

The whole product and codebase looked at again, four days after docs/REVIEW-2026-10.md and the
M10 and M11 work, this time from six chairs at once, each reading the repository at 6f1d5f9 and
running what it could in this container: security and integrity, architecture and cleanliness,
UI and UX (with screenshots in both languages, phone and desktop), intelligence (59 message
probes, 40 date probes, 31 search queries), performance and scalability (query plans, the
bundle, the scale tests), and integration and adoption (the API, the SDK, OAuth, operability,
billing, deep links). Each reviewer wrote findings with file and line and a concrete fix;
this page is what they agreed on, ranked, what was fixed in the same block (commit after this
one) and what remains, with its owner. Nothing here is estimated: every number was produced by
running something (convention 13).

## In one paragraph

Caime is well built and honest about itself. No `any`, one event type shared by server and app,
one error shape, one contract, decisions recorded in unusual detail, 273 of 283 routes reached
by tests, strong auth and OAuth, a business mask applied in one place, private conversations
the server can't read, a public site and app that pass axe in both languages. Where it was weak
was at the edges of what's otherwise strong: a revoked session kept its live socket; one
malformed socket frame could end the process; an under-18's invite link connected any adult who
opened it; the rules that make Caime "understand" read "Have a nice weekend" as a question and
"wasn't approved" as a decision, and nothing measured their precision; the two busiest views of
the business inbox were near-identical words for opposite states; a dozen strings reached
Arabic readers in English; an organization's app never heard that a customer's conversation
was erased; and the heaviest read, the inbox, was fetched whole after every burst of messages.
All of those are fixed below and tested. What remains is either the owner's (a store build, a
penetration test, payment in local currencies, an npm account) or product work that belongs in
its own block (a shorter first five minutes, group fan-out in batches, a replay for webhook
deliveries, the operator's tools for a locked-out person).

## What the six reviews agreed on

Severity: **H** hurts people, money or trust now; **M** costs quality, cost or the next
customer; **L** polish. Each line says where it stands after this block.

### Security and integrity

| | Finding | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H | A live WebSocket outlived its session: sign-out, revocation, a password change, a recovery, a suspension and removing a device only updated the database, so someone holding a stolen session kept receiving every message until the connection dropped (`modules/realtime.ts`) | **Fixed.** Every path that ends a session goes through `endSessions` (`lib/sessions.ts`), which publishes `session.ended` on the bus; the hub on every instance closes that session's sockets (4401) and the session can't reconnect. `messaging.test.ts` |
| H | Invite links bypassed the under-18 rules (R29): any adult who opened a minor's 30-day link was connected at once and landed in a conversation, where `POST /connections/requests` refuses exactly this | **Fixed.** An adult who shares no connection with an under-18 inviter gets `status: 'requested'`: a request the inviter decides on, told as one, no second request on a second open; another under-18, or an adult they know through someone, connects as before. The app says so. `invites.test.ts` |
| M | Web push endpoints were only checked as URLs, so an account could make the server POST a signed request to an internal address on every notification, with no deadline | **Fixed.** The endpoint goes through the webhook address check (https, no private network) and every push has a 10-second deadline |
| M | `GET /calls/ice` made a paid Cloudflare call on every request, unlimited, for anyone signed in | **Fixed.** Credentials are kept per person for half their life and the route is limited to 60 an hour. `calls.test.ts` |
| M | The database URL, password included, was an argument to `pg_dump`, so a failed backup wrote it to the log and `/proc` showed it | **Fixed.** The connection travels as libpq's `PG*` variables (`pgEnv`) |
| M | The six-digit email code could be guessed in parallel bursts: the attempt counter had a lost-update race and no rate limit | **Fixed.** Counted in the database (`attempts + 1`) and ten tries in ten minutes per account |
| M | A report took any message or conversation id, and the operator's view didn't say a message was imported, so a fabricated WhatsApp import read as the reported person's own words beside a one-tap suspend | **Fixed.** A report is refused (404) unless the reporter is in the conversation; `ReportView.message.imported` says so. `moderation.test.ts` |
| M | `trustProxy: true` reads the leftmost `X-Forwarded-For`, so whether per-IP limits can be spoofed depends on whether the proxy chain appends or strips the header | **Open, owner.** Confirm EasyPanel's proxy behaviour and set `TRUST_PROXY` to a hop count if it appends (docs/DEPLOY.md) |
| L | A suggestion accepted twice at once made two tasks; forwarding a file ignored `left_at`; invite tokens appear in request logs; a known handle's sign-in can be kept refused with bad passwords; backups and report reads have no audit entry; concurrent upload chunks aren't locked | **Fixed:** `acceptSuggestion` claims the row under its lock (one task, the second finds it answered); forwarding goes through `membership`, which needs a live seat (now tested); `/i/<token>` and `/v1/invites/<token>` are masked in the request line (`lib/log.ts`); the per-identifier sign-in limit is per address too; `admin.backup_run`, `admin.backups_viewed` and `admin.reports_viewed` are audited; a chunk is appended under the file's row lock |

Strong: the auth plugin (cookie flags, CSRF header, account check, tokens confined to their route
lists), OAuth (PKCE S256, single-use codes, refresh rotation with reuse detection), tokens stored
only as hashes, the business mask in `messageViews` and the bus transform, private conversations
enforced on the server, the webhook SSRF guard, file sniffing and re-encoding, a strict CSP,
Stripe signatures over the raw body, migrations checksummed at boot.

### Intelligence

| | Finding | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H | Pleasantries read as questions ("Will do", "Have a nice weekend", "What a day", "ما شاء الله", "ليش لا"), so the conversation entered Needs you, pushed at the attention level and started a follow-up timer | **Fixed.** Without a question mark, a sentence asks only when an auxiliary is followed by its subject or a question word by a verb; a list of said-but-not-asked phrases in both languages. Golden set |
| H | Negated and questioned decisions were recorded: "The budget wasn't approved." and "Was the budget approved?" both gave the decision "Approved"; "انت موافق؟" too | **Fixed.** A question is never a decision; a negation in the 25 characters before the trigger, or `AR_NEGATIVE_BEFORE`, takes it back; "going with" needs its subject |
| H | Forwarded and quoted words were analysed as the sender's: forwarding "I'll transfer the deposit tomorrow" gave the forwarder a reminder; `He said "I'll send it Monday" but he never did` gave a waiting item at "Quite sure" | **Fixed.** Quoted spans (`"…"`, `“…”`, `«…»`, `> ` lines) are blanked before the rules read, their length kept so indices hold; a forwarded message's analysis is kept for its entities and drafts no suggestion |
| H | Sentences split at every `.`: "$2.5k", "v2.1", "sam@data-c.com" and URLs broke titles and lost due dates | **Fixed.** A sentence ends at punctuation followed by a space or the end, or at a line's end |
| M | English false positives: "Let me know when you're free" (waiting), "please ignore my last message" (task), "Can you believe it?" (task), "Order #48213 shipped" (task); the negation "I won't be able to send it, but I'll call you tomorrow" lost the promise | **Fixed** (the first four and the clause-local negation); "Will do" and "On it" as vague commitments wait |
| M | Arabic triggers fired on third persons and non-requests: "أخوي راح يجيب" (my brother will), "رح يمطر" (it'll rain), "ممكن أتأخر" (I may be late), "شو بدك تاكل" (what do you want to eat), "الله يبارك فيك" | **Fixed.** `راح`/`رح` take first-person prefixes only; `ممكن`, `فيك` and `بدك` count only before a second-person verb, and `بدك` never after a question word |
| M | The AI agent's own replies went through the rule engine and became decision cards for the customer, which a customer can steer by prompt injection | **Fixed.** `suggestBusiness` drafts nothing for the customer from an agent's or a bot's message |
| M | No golden set or precision measure; the 52 behaviour tests all passed with the false positives above | **Fixed.** 127 labelled lines, precision and recall per label and language, floors that fail CI and print each wrong line (question ≥ 0.85, request, commitment and decision ≥ 0.90 precision; recall ≥ 0.80; due dates ≥ 0.85) |
| M | Dates: "last Friday" lands in the future, "yesterday", "the 12th", "بعد أسبوعين" and "الساعة 5 ونص" unread, "dinner at 8" is 08:00; an edit is never analysed again; a vague promise re-dates the latest unrelated suggestion; search rules misread "photos from last week" as a person and block the model; the search box can call the model on every typing pause; same-name duplicates offered | **Mostly fixed.** The recent past ("last Friday", "yesterday", "last night", "last week") reads as past and is never a due date; a day of the month ("by the 12th") is this month while ahead, else next; Arabic durations ("بعد أسبوعين", "خلال 3 أيام") and clock words ("5 ونص", "إلا ربع"); a bare hour after dinner, drinks or tonight is the evening's ("tonight at 8" is 20:00, "tomorrow morning at 10" stays 10:00); an edited message is read again (`analyseText`, `afterEdit`: its flags and dates, its new suggestions once each by fingerprint). Then the rest: a vague promise re-dates only the item it answers (a reply, or the one open within two days; `absorbVague`), a time at the end of a search is the days to search, never a person (`splitPeriod`, `query.period`), the model reads a sentence only once typing settles or on Enter (`understand=1`), and two people with the same two-word name are offered as one only with a place or a nickname they share (0.6 against `OFFER_AT` 0.65) |

Strong: every suggestion quotes its source and says how sure it is in words; the model widens
the fields the rules fill and never the scope; Arabic dialect depth no competitor has by rules;
DST and workweeks right; business masking covers transcript, actions and suggestions; minors kept
from AI and the agent.

## Method

Six reviews ran in parallel from the same commit, each with the full repository, a database,
the production bundle and the scale tests, and wrote to a scratch file: security (2 high, 6
medium, 6 low), architecture (3 high, 7 medium, 8 low), intelligence (5 high, 11 medium),
UX (5 high, 9 medium), performance (4 high, 10 medium), integration (5 high, 10 medium).
Overlaps were merged above; a finding one reviewer rated differently from another keeps the
higher rating. The individual reports are not committed: this page is the record, and each fix
cites its test.
