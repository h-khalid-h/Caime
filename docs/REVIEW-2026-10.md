# Caime, reviewed: 2026-10-01

The whole product looked at again, one day and a plan (M9) after docs/REVIEW-2026-09.md, before
the next block of work: not the code alone, but what a visitor, a person, a clinic, a buyer, a
regulator and an operator each meet. Facts come from the repository at c6e564f, from production
(https://caime.datac.com, at the same commit) and from running things in this container; nothing
here is estimated. Refinements that follow from it are R53 and R54 in docs/PRODUCT-REVIEW.md;
the plan is M10 in docs/ROADMAP.md, and the owner's checklist is at the end of this page.

## In one paragraph

Everything the last review asked for that could be built without the owner's accounts is built
and verified: backups, a readable web and a public site, Pro that sells the wedge's depth, the
WhatsApp import and a 4.8-second contextual invite, email and moderation, operators by name,
resource budgets, and since then Caime's own calendar with bookings and the meeting-aware
notifications (R51). The product is now two products that share one model: a messenger for
people whose family, work and customers live in one place, and a business messaging tool for an
organization that proves who it is, answers as itself, lets its AI agent book appointments and
plugs in its tools. The second has the clearer buyer and brings its own network (a clinic brings
its patients), which is where the plan below leads. What stands between Caime and that buyer is
not features: it is that the interface speaks only English where the first customers speak
Arabic, that there is still no app in a store, that the live site's contact address is on a
domain the owner doesn't yet hold, that nobody can reset a password without recovery codes, that
the price is in euros by card only, and that a clinic's lawyer has nothing to sign. Each of
those is listed with its fix; most are the owner's to unblock, and all are cheap next to what is
already built.

## Findings by dimension

Severity: **P0** hurts people or the business now; **P1** blocks winning the first customers;
**P2** quality and polish.

### Strategy and the first customers

- The last review called the wedge "built and verified end to end on the web". It still is,
  and a layer has been added: an organization now has a verified page, a shared inbox, an AI
  agent that answers from its own text and books from its open slots, bookable hours, apps with
  signed webhooks, updates to followers, insights and a plan it can buy. That is a product a
  clinic, a shop or a school can be sold, and one whose every customer becomes a person on Caime
  with the wedge in their pocket. The consumer messenger needs both sides to join; the business
  tool brings the other side with it. **The plan leads with organizations** (R53), in one place
  and one kind first, and treats the consumer product as what their customers get.
- **P1** The first customers named in docs/COMPETITIVE.md (where WhatsApp is already used for
  business: the Middle East and North Africa first) read Arabic, and the interface has no Arabic:
  every string is English in the code, and there is no way to choose a language. The right-to-left
  work (R21, R31) made messages and layouts ready; the words aren't. This is the largest gap
  between the product and its stated market, and it is build work, not credentials (R54).
- **P1** Their customers are on WhatsApp. The one candidate from the brainstorm review the
  roadmap keeps, an organization taking its WhatsApp Business messages into the Business inbox
  through Meta's Cloud API, is what makes "move your clinic to Caime" a day's decision rather than
  a year's. ⛔ A Meta business account and its verification, the owner's; the server side is a
  channel into `sendMessage` once it exists.
- The product metrics for the plan exist (`lib/product-metrics.ts`: activation, 7- and 28-day
  retention, invites by people and by organizations), so the beachhead can be watched from day
  one. Nothing is in them yet: there are no customers to count.

### Reach: web, phone, language

- Good: a visitor reads the landing page, five site pages, an organization's page and a person's
  public page without scripts, each with cards for link previews, in 1.4 s on a throttled phone,
  at 100 on every Lighthouse category. The site's copy is one voice: sentence case, spec sheets,
  every number read from the code.
- **P1** Still no app in a store (unchanged since 2026-09-30): `eas.json` and the store identity
  (`me.cai.app`) are written, native bundles export, the permission strings are in `app.json`,
  and nothing has run on a phone. ⛔ Apple and Google developer accounts and `EXPO_TOKEN`.
  Ringing a closed phone (VoIP push) is not built and can't be tried before that.
- **P1** The app's own first screen costs a mid-range phone 4.2 s to its largest paint and 440 ms
  of blocking (sign-in, Lighthouse mobile preset, local bundle), against the 2.0 s the
  competitive targets set. The public pages dodge this by carrying no script; the app can't.
  The entry chunk is 219.9 KB and the shared chunk 127.6 KB over the wire; what loads before the
  first screen is the next thing to measure and cut (`e2e/launch-cost.spec.ts` lists it).
- **P2** `/healthz` without the `/v1` prefix answers the app's HTML, since the server serves the
  app to anything that asks for HTML; a monitor pointed at the wrong path would read "up" from a
  page. The documented path is `/v1/readyz`; say so once more in DEPLOY.md's alert section.

### Trust, legal and the clinic's lawyer

- **P0** The contact address on every public page, in the privacy policy, the terms, About and
  the push subscription (`CONTACT_EMAIL`, `VAPID_SUBJECT`) is `hello@cai.me`, decoded from
  production's own pages today, and `cai.me` is parked for sale at Afternic (ROADMAP M8): whoever
  buys the domain receives Caime's privacy requests, abuse reports and support mail. Until the
  domain is the owner's, the address must be one on a domain they hold. ⛔ The owner sets
  `CONTACT_EMAIL` and `VAPID_SUBJECT` on the `caime` service (docs/DEPLOY.md now says which);
  the code needs nothing.
- **P1** Nobody on production can confirm an email address or reset a forgotten password:
  `SMTP_URL` is unset (DEPLOY.md's checklist), so R48's flows are built and idle, and recovery
  codes are the only way back. For a consumer product that is a steady leak of accounts. ⛔ An
  SMTP account (Postmark, Resend, Brevo: the free tiers cover the first months).
- **P1** Selling to a clinic makes Caime a processor of its customers' conversations, health
  details among them (special-category data under GDPR, which applies to DATA C OÜ wherever its
  customers are). There is nothing an organization's lawyer can sign (no data processing
  agreement, no list of sub-processors in one place, no retention statement from the
  organization's side), and nothing the organization can do for its customers' rights: no
  export of its own conversations, no erasure on a customer's request, no owner-set retention
  for business conversations. The privacy page names Anthropic, Stripe and Cloudflare in prose
  and never the host. R54 fixes the product side (export, erasure, retention, a sub-processor
  list the page and the agreement read from the same constants); the agreement's text and the
  lawyer's review of all three documents are ⛔ the owner's.
- Good: the privacy and terms pages are Caime's own and checked against the code; AI is off
  until an adult turns it on and never reads a private conversation; the agent never answers a
  minor; the security page says what isn't hidden. SECURITY.md lists 15 gaps honestly; the
  pentest is still ⛔ a third party's.
- **P2** `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN` beside `frame-ancestors 'none'`: the CSP wins in every
  browser that matters, but the header should say `DENY` so the two agree.

### Monetization

- Plans and prices are live and bought in the app (Pro €6 or €60, Business €29 or €290; Stripe
  live). The pricing page reads them from Stripe. The wedge is never counted, as R23 says.
- **P1** The price is in euros, by card, only. The first customers pay in pounds, dirhams and
  riyals, often without an international card, and a clinic that can't pay can't buy. Stripe
  presents prices in those currencies for an EU account, by lookup key; local payment methods
  (Fawry, mada, STC Pay, Vodafone Cash) are each a Stripe or partner decision with fees. ⛔ The
  owner's decision on currencies and methods; the code takes a currency dimension on
  `caime_<plan>_<interval>` and shows the visitor's.
- **P1** Tax: nothing in billing or DEPLOY.md mentions VAT. An Estonian company selling Pro to
  consumers in the EU owes VAT where they are (OSS), and Business to companies elsewhere depends
  on their status. Stripe Tax is a switch on the account; the price shown must then say what it
  includes. ⛔ The owner's, with their accountant.
- **P2** The gap between an organization's Free (3 people, 1 app, 50 agent answers a day) and
  Business at €29 (100 people, 25 apps, 2,000 answers) is wide for a two-chair clinic or a shop,
  which is exactly who comes first. Whether a smaller tier (say 10 people, 5 apps, 500 answers)
  earns more than it costs in support is the owner's call; the plan file takes one in a line.

### Product and the wedge

- The wedge's moment, connect in three taps and a first message, is designed (R3), measured
  (4.8 s from an invite link to a first message in E2E, the typing not counted) and offered on
  day one with three first lines fitted to the relationship. Single-player value is real now:
  actions, waiting, the calendar, bookings for an organization, insights on Pro.
- The relationship does what the PRD promised, visibly: the label on the header, "Only you see
  this label", the reason under every inbox placement, rules by sphere accepted in one tap at
  onboarding with the local work week (Sun–Thu in Egypt), work held outside hours, in quiet
  hours and now in a meeting, read receipts both ways only, profile fields by sphere, "busy or
  free" by relationship.
- **P2** Settings are scattered where the model is one: quiet hours live under Automations
  while "Work waits until a meeting ends" lives under Notifications; the help page sends people
  to both. One place, Notifications and priorities, for everything about who reaches you when.
- **P2** A person's profile says "Verified at Nile Dental" and then shows a Nile Dental chip
  beneath it: the same fact twice. One of them goes.
- **P2** The conversation's details panel shows disappearing messages as a permanent list of
  six options; every other setting is a row that opens a choice. On a phone the list pushes
  "Coming up" and "Open" off the first screen of the sheet.
- **P2** On a 390-px phone the Actions segmented control truncates to "Asked…", "Wait… 1",
  "Calen…": five segments don't fit. Two rows, a scrolling row, or shorter words ("Asked",
  "Waiting", "Done", "Calendar" with the counts as badges).
- **P2** An organization's public page ends in "Message Nile Dental on Caime", which signs the
  visitor up and returns them to the page, where one more tap writes. The invite link lands in
  the conversation; the organization's page should too (R53 makes it the clinic's own link, on
  its door and its receipts, with a QR code the app makes).

### Engineering, reliability, operations

- Sound and measured: one image, CI with E2E on the production bundle, migrations under a lock,
  jobs claimed with `SKIP LOCKED`, realtime fan-out over NOTIFY, backups dumped and checked
  daily, `/metrics` with queue depth and oldest-job age, `pnpm audit` in CI, alert rules written
  in DEPLOY.md, resource budgets with numbers (docs/RESOURCES.md).
- **P1** Still nothing watches production from outside: no uptime check, no alert receiver, and
  backups stay on the box (the off-host copy is built, ⛔ a bucket and keys). A lost host still
  loses everything; a stuck worker is still invisible until someone looks. ⛔ The owner's: a
  bucket, and a Grafana Cloud or Better Stack account pointed at `/v1/readyz` and `/metrics`.
- **P2** The EasyPanel API answered this container 401 today, so what production's service sets
  could not be read from here; DEPLOY.md's checklist (2026-09-30) is the record used above.
- **P2** Branch protection on `main` and the first-review secret are still ⛔ the owner's (M9).

### UX, UI, brand and copy

- The screens are calm and consistent: one accent, relationship chips in their sphere colours,
  mono labels on spec sheets, sentence case throughout, characters only where there is something
  to celebrate or nothing to show. Dark mode is complete where photographed (2 of 91 screens).
- **P2** The screenshot suite photographs two screens in dark mode; the competitive targets ask
  for every primary flow in light and dark on both sizes each release. Add the dark pass to the
  E2E screenshots (a theme switch per test is cheap) so a regression in dark shows.
- **P2** Not yet measured: layout at 320 px and at 200% text size, and a screen-reader pass on
  a device (listed since M9).
- **P2** Cloudflare's email obfuscation rewrites `hello@…` on the public pages into
  `[email protected]` for any reader without JavaScript (answer engines, text browsers, the
  `curl` used here), since the pages carry no script of their own. Turn Scrape Shield's email
  obfuscation off for the zone, or write the address as text beside the link.
- **P2** The landing page carries two `description` metas (the template's and the page's);
  search engines take the first. Drop the template's when a page sets its own.
- Copy on the live pages reads as one person wrote it, which matters; two lines of the help page
  have aged ("Quiet hours are in You → Automations" moves with the setting; "not in the phone
  apps" is true until the store builds and should then go).

## The plan (M10 in docs/ROADMAP.md)

Ordered by what each unblocks, the owner's steps first because they are cheapest and gate the
rest; the build work runs in parallel with them.

1. **P0, the owner, today.** `CONTACT_EMAIL` and `VAPID_SUBJECT` on a domain the owner holds
   (or buy `cai.me`, which also unblocks M8's link and passkeys). An SMTP account and
   `SMTP_URL`, so addresses confirm and passwords reset. A bucket for backups and files. An
   uptime check and an alert receiver. Each is minutes on a panel and a few euros a month.
2. **P1, build: Caime in Arabic (R54).** Every interface string into one table per package
   with English as the source, a language setting (device language by default, `safeLocale`
   deciding), Arabic as the first translation with the product's own voice reviewed by a native
   speaker, `lang` and `dir` on the web, the remaining 14 physical styles made logical, and
   the public site in both. The AI translate feature (PRD §23) already carries Arabic; this is
   the product itself. Measured by the E2E suite run in Arabic with screenshots.
3. **P1, build: the organization's own door (R53).** An organization's page lands a new
   customer in the conversation as an invite does, a QR code and a short link the app makes for
   the door, the receipt and the bio, "Verified" explained to the customer in one line, and the
   organization's onboarding written for a clinic (hours, bookings, the agent's text, the team)
   in the order a clinic sets them up. Measured: link-open to first customer message, as the
   invite is.
4. **P1, build: what a clinic's lawyer needs (R54).** An organization exports its own
   conversations, erases a customer's on request, sets a retention for business conversations,
   and sees its sub-processors; the privacy page and a data processing agreement read the same
   list from the same constants. ⛔ The agreement's text and a lawyer's review of all three
   documents are the owner's; the product side ships first so there is something to review.
5. **P1, the owner's decisions:** the phone (Apple and Google accounts, then the development
   build on the owner's iPhone, TestFlight and Play internal testing: everything else is
   written); currencies, local payment methods and Stripe Tax; WhatsApp Business through Meta's
   Cloud API; a smaller organization tier. Each is a line in the owner's checklist with its cost.
6. **P1, build: the app's first screen under 2 s.** Measure what the entry chunk carries
   (`expo export --source-maps`, `scripts/bundle-budget.mjs`), move what the first screen doesn't
   draw behind `lazyPart`, and record LCP on the mobile preset in docs/RESOURCES.md until it is
   under 2.0 s.
7. **P2, polish that shows:** quiet hours into Notifications and priorities; the duplicate
   organization line on a profile; disappearing messages as one row; the Actions segments on a
   phone; `X-Frame-Options: DENY`; one description meta; the two aged help lines; the dark pass
   in the screenshot suite; the 320-px and 200% checks; the screen-reader pass once a phone
   exists.

What this review did not do: sign up on production (it would write to the live database), read
production's environment (the panel's API refused this container), run a load test again (the
numbers in docs/RESOURCES.md are from 2026-09-30 and the code paths they measure are unchanged),
or test on a phone (there is none). Those are listed under M10 as measurements to take, not
assumed.
